This policy explains what information "Shh! UFO" (the "Game") processes and how.

We do not offer accounts and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or similar information.
Your game progress (UFO size, logbook, settings, selfie album, etc.) is **stored only on your device** and is never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.
(A "save code" you export is kept and shared by you; it is not sent to us.)

| --- | --- | --- |

### 3. International transfers

These providers are located outside Korea, so information is transferred abroad (to the United States) over encrypted connections when the Game loads or shows ads, looks up products, processes a purchase or restores purchases. Retention follows each provider's policy and applicable law.
Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy · RevenueCat: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

### 4. Retention

- Game progress on your device: until you delete the app or reset the game.
- Purchase records may be retained by the stores and the purchase-processing service for the periods required by law (in Korea: 5 years for payment/delivery records, 3 years for complaint/dispute records).
- Support emails we receive: deleted [1 year] after the request is resolved.

### 5. Personalised ads and your choices

- **iOS**: you can decline tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt, and change it later in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
- **Android**: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings › Privacy (or Google) › Ads.
- **EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions that require consent**: a consent form appears on first launch, and you can change your choice at any time from "Ad privacy settings" in the game's settings.
- Declining tracking or personalised ads does not limit any game feature (you may still see non-personalised ads).

### 6. Children

The Game is intended for users **aged 12 and over** and is not directed at children. Ads are limited to the Teen (T) content rating or lower.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14 (under 13 in the United States). If you are a parent or guardian and believe such information was processed, contact us and we will help delete it.

### 7. Your rights

You may request access to, correction or deletion of, or restriction of processing of your personal information by emailing [contact@example.com].
Because we do not hold information that identifies you, please include your store order number (receipt) for purchase-related requests.
You can also exercise your rights directly with Google and RevenueCat as described in their policies.

### 8. Security

All communication with third-party services is encrypted (HTTPS), and we process only the minimum information needed.

### 9. Changes

We will announce changes on this page and in the Game at least 7 days before they take effect (30 days for changes that reduce your rights).
